Shockwave Therapy · Surrey, BC

Focused acoustic-wave treatment within a rehabilitation plan

Shockwave therapy, also called extracorporeal shockwave therapy, uses mechanical acoustic waves delivered to a targeted area. It may be considered for selected musculoskeletal conditions as part of a broader rehabilitation plan.

About the service

Shockwave Therapy

Your physiotherapist determines whether shockwave therapy is appropriate after reviewing your symptoms, history and physical findings. The treatment area and settings are selected according to your presentation and tolerance.

Treatment can feel intense or uncomfortable for some people, but settings can be adjusted. Temporary tenderness may occur. Your physiotherapist will review relevant precautions before treatment.

When it may help

Care built around your goals.

Every treatment is selected in context. Your physiotherapist will discuss whether a particular approach is appropriate for you.

01

Targeted treatment

The applicator delivers acoustic waves to a selected treatment area based on the clinical assessment.

02

Commonly considered for

It may be considered for selected persistent tendon and other musculoskeletal problems when clinically appropriate.

03

Rehab matters

Shockwave may be combined with progressive exercise, load management and education rather than used as a stand-alone solution.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is shockwave therapy painful?

It can feel intense or uncomfortable, particularly over sensitive areas. Treatment intensity can be adjusted to your tolerance.

How many treatments will I need?

There is no single treatment schedule for every condition. Your physiotherapist will discuss an appropriate plan based on your assessment and response.

Is shockwave used by itself?

It may be used as one component of care. Exercise, activity modification and other rehabilitation strategies may also be appropriate.
