Questions

Before your visit.

Do you assess foot conditions and provide insoles?

Yes. Hands-On Physio now offers foot condition assessments and makes insoles, with modifications based on your assessment and individual needs.

How are insole modifications decided?

Modifications are guided by the findings of your foot assessment and your individual needs. We discuss the options with you as part of your plan.

How do I arrange a foot assessment?

Call Hands-On Physio at (778) 998-1600. Our team can answer questions about the service and help you arrange an appointment.
