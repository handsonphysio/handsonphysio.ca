For injured workers

Focused care for
work-related injuries.

Hands-On Physio provides individualized physiotherapy for workers recovering from eligible workplace musculoskeletal and orthopedic injuries.

Each visit is centered on your current symptoms, physical function, work demands, recovery goals, and the requirements of your WorkSafeBC claim.

Common presentations

We can help with
many workplace injuries.

01

Back & neck injuries

Assessment and rehabilitation for work-related pain, stiffness, movement limitations, and functional restrictions.

02

Shoulder & arm injuries

Individualized care for strains, sprains, reduced mobility, weakness, and activity-related limitations.

03

Knee & leg injuries

Rehabilitation for lower-extremity injuries affecting walking, lifting, squatting, stairs, and workplace tasks.

04

Sprains & strains

Progressive treatment and exercise aimed at restoring comfortable movement, strength, and function.

05

Repetitive strain injuries

Care that considers repetitive work demands, movement patterns, physical capacity, and graded activity.

06

Post-injury rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation to help restore physical function and support a safe progression toward work activities.

Your rehabilitation pathway

Assess.
Treat.
Restore & empower.

Our approach is designed around your individual presentation rather than a one-size-fits-all protocol.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Understand the injury, symptoms, movement, physical function, and work-related limitations.

  2. 02

    Address functional goals and work demands as appropriate, with collaboration across the rehabilitation team.

Frequently asked questions

WorkSafeBC
questions.

Is Hands-On Physio a contracted WorkSafeBC provider? +

Yes. Hands-On Physio is a contracted WorkSafeBC physiotherapy provider for eligible musculoskeletal and orthopedic physiotherapy services.

Do I need a WorkSafeBC claim number? +

If you have a claim number, provide it when booking. If you have reported the injury but have not received the number yet, contact the clinic and provide it as soon as you receive it.

Can I have an assessment if my claim is still pending? +

WorkSafeBC states that contracted physiotherapists can perform an initial assessment within 60 days of the injury for a worker with an accepted or pending claim. Treatment beyond the assessment requires the claim to be accepted.

Does WorkSafeBC pay for my physiotherapy? +

Eligible health care benefits are covered according to the worker's claim and applicable WorkSafeBC policies. As a contracted provider, Hands-On Physio follows WorkSafeBC's applicable billing requirements.

Can physiotherapy help with return to work? +

Yes. Rehabilitation can address physical function and work-related activity goals and can support a safe progression toward work when appropriate.
