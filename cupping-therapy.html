Cupping Therapy · Surrey, BC

Supportive treatment. Individualized application.

Cupping therapy uses cups placed on the skin to create suction. At Hands-On Physio, cupping may be considered as an adjunct to physiotherapy based on your symptoms, examination and treatment goals.

About the service

Cupping Therapy

Your physiotherapist discusses whether cupping is appropriate for you and explains what to expect. Cups are applied for a selected period and the response is monitored.

Temporary circular marks or bruising-like discoloration are common after cupping and can last for several days. Your physiotherapist can explain expected effects and relevant precautions.

When it may help

Care built around your goals.

Every treatment is selected in context. Your physiotherapist will discuss whether a particular approach is appropriate for you.

01

Muscle-focused care

Cupping may be used alongside other techniques when muscular tightness or local discomfort is part of the clinical picture.

02

03

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Will cupping leave marks?

It can. Temporary circular marks or discoloration are a common response to suction and usually fade over time.

Is cupping a replacement for physiotherapy?

No. When used, it is an adjunct to a broader assessment and rehabilitation plan.

Can I request cupping?

You can discuss it with your physiotherapist. They will determine whether it is appropriate based on your presentation and goals.
