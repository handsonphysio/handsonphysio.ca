About the service

Exercise Therapy

Your physiotherapist identifies the movements and physical capacities most relevant to your goals. Exercises are then selected at an appropriate starting level.

As your tolerance improves, the program can be progressed through changes in resistance, repetitions, range, speed, complexity or functional demands.

When it may help

Care built around your goals.

Every treatment is selected in context. Your physiotherapist will discuss whether a particular approach is appropriate for you.

01

Strength & capacity

Progressive strengthening can help rebuild capacity after injury, inactivity or periods of reduced loading.

02

Mobility & control

Exercises can target range of motion, coordination, balance and movement control where appropriate.

03

Functional goals

Training can be progressed toward work tasks, recreation, sport or everyday activities that matter to you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Will exercises be customized?

Yes. Exercises are selected and progressed according to your goals, current capacity, symptoms and functional demands.

Can exercise therapy help after an injury?

Progressive exercise is commonly used during rehabilitation to restore strength, movement and functional capacity when appropriate.

Do I need gym equipment?

Not necessarily. Programs can be designed around the equipment available in the clinic and what you can safely perform at home or work.
