ICBC INJURY REHABILITATION

Physiotherapy after a motor-vehicle crash

A collision can affect your neck, back, shoulders, hips, knees and other areas of your body. Symptoms may include pain, stiffness, reduced mobility, weakness, headaches or difficulty returning to normal activities.

At Hands-On Physio, we provide individualized physiotherapy focused on restoring movement, improving physical function and helping you progress toward your everyday activities, work and personal goals.

OUR APPROACH

One patient at a time.

Your physiotherapy appointment is dedicated to you. We take time to assess your condition, understand how your injury affects daily life and build a rehabilitation plan around your needs.

Treatment may include hands-on techniques, therapeutic exercise, mobility work, strengthening, movement retraining and functional rehabilitation, depending on your assessment.

ICBC COVERAGE

Direct billing available

Hands-On Physio can direct bill ICBC for eligible physiotherapy services associated with an ICBC claim.

Under ICBC's current Enhanced Care program, eligible customers can access pre-approved physiotherapy treatment during the first 12 weeks after a crash. ICBC currently lists up to 25 pre-approved physiotherapy treatments during this initial period, subject to applicable rules.

FUNCTIONAL REHABILITATION

Recovery means getting back to life.

Physiotherapy is not only about reducing symptoms. We also work toward restoring the physical abilities you need for everyday life.

Depending on your needs, rehabilitation may include progressive strengthening, mobility exercises, balance training, movement retraining and functional activities.

If your injury affects your ability to work, appropriate functional goals can be incorporated into your rehabilitation plan.

ICBC FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Hands-On Physio direct bill ICBC?

Yes. Hands-On Physio offers direct billing to ICBC for eligible ICBC physiotherapy services.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

For ICBC pre-approved treatment during the first 12 weeks after a crash, ICBC states that you do not need a referral from a family physician or nurse practitioner. You will need your ICBC claim number and Personal Health Number.

How many physiotherapy treatments are pre-approved?

ICBC currently states that 25 physiotherapy treatments are pre-approved during the first 12 weeks following a crash, subject to applicable ICBC rules.

What if I need treatment after the initial period?

If additional treatment is clinically recommended, your physiotherapist can work with ICBC according to the applicable treatment-plan and approval requirements.

Do you provide one-on-one physiotherapy?

Yes. One patient at a time is central to the Hands-On Physio approach, with your assessment, treatment and rehabilitation focused on your needs.
