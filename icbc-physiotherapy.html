ICBC INJURY REHABILITATION

Physiotherapy after a motor-vehicle crash

A collision can affect your neck, back, shoulders, hips, knees and other areas of your body. Symptoms may include pain, stiffness, reduced mobility, weakness, headaches or difficulty returning to your normal activities.

At Hands-On Physio, we provide individualized physiotherapy focused on restoring movement, improving physical function and helping you progress toward your everyday activities, work and personal goals.

OUR APPROACH

One patient at a time.

Your physiotherapy appointment is dedicated to you. We take the time to assess your condition, understand how your injury affects your daily life and build a rehabilitation plan around your specific needs.

Treatment may combine hands-on techniques, therapeutic exercise, mobility work, strengthening, movement retraining and functional rehabilitation.

COMMON MOTOR-VEHICLE INJURIES

Injuries we can help rehabilitate

01

Neck & whiplash injuries

Neck pain, stiffness, reduced range of motion, muscle tension and movement-related symptoms following a collision.

02

Back injuries

Back pain, stiffness, reduced mobility and difficulty with sitting, standing, lifting or other everyday activities.

03

Shoulder injuries

Shoulder pain, restricted movement, weakness and difficulty reaching, lifting or performing daily tasks.

04

Hip & pelvic injuries

Pain or movement limitations affecting walking, transfers, exercise and other functional activities.

05

Knee & lower-limb injuries

Knee, ankle and lower-extremity symptoms that affect walking, stairs, balance or physical activity.

06

General musculoskeletal injuries

Sprains, strains, soft-tissue injuries and other musculoskeletal problems following a motor-vehicle collision.

ICBC COVERAGE

Direct billing available

Hands-On Physio can direct bill ICBC for eligible physiotherapy services associated with an ICBC claim.

Under ICBC's current Enhanced Care program, eligible customers have access to pre-approved physiotherapy treatment during the first 12 weeks following a crash.

ICBC currently lists up to 25 pre-approved physiotherapy treatments during this initial period, subject to applicable ICBC rules.

FUNCTIONAL REHABILITATION

Recovery means getting back to life.

Physiotherapy is not only about reducing symptoms. We also work toward restoring the physical abilities you need for everyday life.

Depending on your individual needs, rehabilitation may include progressive strengthening, mobility exercises, balance training, movement retraining and functional activities.

If your injury affects your ability to work, we can incorporate appropriate functional goals into your rehabilitation plan.

ICBC FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can Hands-On Physio direct bill ICBC?

Yes. Hands-On Physio offers direct billing to ICBC for eligible ICBC physiotherapy services.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

For ICBC pre-approved treatment during the first 12 weeks after a crash, ICBC states that you do not need a referral from a family physician or nurse practitioner. You will need your ICBC claim number and Personal Health Number.

How many physiotherapy treatments are covered?

ICBC currently states that 25 physiotherapy treatments are pre-approved during the first 12 weeks following a crash, subject to applicable ICBC rules.

Can I start physiotherapy before seeing my doctor?

Under ICBC's current early-access rules, eligible customers can access pre-approved physiotherapy during the initial 12-week period without a referral from a family physician or nurse practitioner.

What if I need physiotherapy after the initial treatment period?

If additional treatment is clinically recommended, your physiotherapist can work with ICBC according to the applicable treatment-plan and approval requirements.

Do you provide one-on-one physiotherapy?

Yes. One patient at a time is central to the Hands-On Physio approach. Your physiotherapy appointment is focused on your assessment, treatment and rehabilitation.
