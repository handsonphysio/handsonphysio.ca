Physiotherapy · Surrey, BC

Individualized care for how you move

Physiotherapy combines clinical assessment, hands-on care, movement and exercise to help you understand your problem and work toward meaningful functional goals. At Hands-On Physio, your appointment is focused on one patient at a time.

About the service

Physiotherapy

Your first visit begins with a conversation about your symptoms, goals and relevant history, followed by a physical assessment. Your physiotherapist then explains the findings and discusses treatment options with you.

Treatment may include hands-on techniques, mobility work, exercise therapy, education and other appropriate physiotherapy techniques. Your plan is adjusted as you progress.

When it may help

Care built around your goals.

Every treatment is selected in context. Your physiotherapist will discuss whether a particular approach is appropriate for you.

01

Pain & movement problems

Assessment and treatment for common musculoskeletal concerns involving the back, neck, shoulders, hips, knees, ankles and other areas.

02

Injury rehabilitation

A structured plan can help restore mobility, strength, confidence and everyday function after injury or surgery when appropriate.

03

Return to activity

Progressive exercise and functional training can support a safe return to work, sport, recreation and daily activities.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a referral?

For many private physiotherapy visits, a physician referral is not required. Coverage requirements can vary by insurer or program, so check your plan when applicable.

What happens at the first appointment?

Your physiotherapist discusses your concerns and goals, performs an assessment, explains the findings and starts an appropriate treatment plan.

Will my treatment include exercises?

Exercise is commonly used when appropriate, but your plan may also include education, hands-on techniques and other physiotherapy approaches.
